EduBridge BD: journey to screens

The journey runs down the left as numbered steps. Every screen hangs off the step it serves. Two failures are visible without reading anything: a step with no screen attached to it, and a screen with no line back to the spine.

Journey: the parent who pays, on a sub-15,000 taka Android, evening. Sources: PRODUCT.md, brief-v3-interrogated.md, critique-notes.md, booking-screen.html.

What the parent does Screens that serve it
1 Decides the child needs a tutor For one named subject, HSC Physics, not "tutoring".
Happens before the product. No screen needed, and that is correct.
2 Looks for tutors who teach it Wants a shortlist small enough to compare.
Subject search Tutors for one subject. Serves step 2.
Search results, empty No tutors for that subject yet. Serves step 2.
3 Judges whether the tutor is real Looking for who checked the qualifications. Branch: leaves and asks a neighbour instead
Tutor profile Who this person is, and who verified them. Serves step 3. Open question: nobody has named the issuer of the badge, so half of this step has no screen yet.
4 Works out what one session costs Wants the total in BDT, not an hourly rate to multiply.
Booking requestbuilt The session, the total, one CTA. Serves steps 4 and 5. This is booking-screen.html.
5 Sends the booking request Needs to understand this is a request, not a purchase. Branch: abandons if the wording promised an instant booking
6 Waits for the tutor to confirm Wants to know the request arrived, and when the answer comes. Branch: nothing arrives, and the parent gives up quietly
No screen for this step Nothing serves step 6 Not in the build, not in the brief. The CTA promises an answer in about two hours and there is nowhere to go and see it. The parent has paid nothing yet, so leaving costs them nothing.
7 Pays Card through SSLCOMMERZ for the card minority. bKash or Nagad for everyone else: leave the site, switch app, PIN, OTP.
Payment method choice Card or mobile money. Serves step 7.
Mobile money handoff What is about to happen, and what to bring back. Serves step 7.
8 Proves the payment Types the transaction ID back in by hand, then waits for verification that is not instant.
Transaction ID entry The hand-typed TrxID. Serves step 8.
Verification pending Waiting, with a time on it. Serves step 8.
Payment failed, five states Wrong TrxID, expired OTP, insufficient balance, duplicate payment, timeout. Serves step 8.
9 Knows the session is booked Time, subject, and how the session will happen.
Booking confirmed The session exists. Serves step 9.
No step. No line. Pay with bKash panel, inside the booking request screen It collects a payment number at step 5. Payment is step 7, after the tutor confirms, and the panel's own note says no payment is charged yet. Either the panel moves to step 7, or the journey is wrong about when money is taken.

Ended well, for the parent. The money reached a person whose documents somebody checked, and the total paid is the total that was shown.

The same fault, smaller. The session details on the built screen show a Format row reading "Online (video call)". brief-v3-interrogated.md puts video calling out of scope, so step 9 cannot state the format truthfully yet.

Still open. Who issues the verified badge. Whether Nagad is in MVP, which turns the payment method choice from one option into two. Whether search is in the first build: PRODUCT.md lists it in MVP, brief-v3-interrogated.md puts search and discovery out. Steps 2 and 9 depend on which is true.
